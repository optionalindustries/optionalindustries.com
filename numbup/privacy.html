This Privacy Policy describes how Optional Industries ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you participate in the Closed Alpha test of numbup ("the App").

1. Scope

This policy applies only to the numbup Closed Alpha test build, distributed exclusively via Apple TestFlight and Google Play Internal Testing to a limited group of invited testers. It does not apply to any future public release, which will be governed by an updated policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Technical processors who handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): Database, authentication, and realtime infrastructure. Project hosted in the EU (Ireland, AWS eu-west-1). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): Crash and performance reporting — currently inactive in Alpha builds.
  • Apple / Google: Distribution via TestFlight / Internal Testing. Subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All game data is stored on EU-based servers (Supabase, Ireland).
  • Access is protected via Row Level Security — each account can only read its own data; chat messages are readable by all testers by design.
  • Server-side writes go through validated endpoints only; save data is integrity-protected (HMAC).
  • Session tokens are stored in the App's private storage area on your device. They are not encrypted at rest beyond what the operating system provides for app-private files; storing them in the platform keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) is planned before public release.
  • All network traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS).

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing or restrict it
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for Austria: dsb.gv.at)

Deletion and export are available in the app: open your profile, then Settings → Account, where you can Delete Account or Export My Data. Export gives you a machine-readable JSON copy of your data via the clipboard (a portable format, GDPR Art. 20). In-app deletion takes effect immediately and is irreversible — deleting your account removes all server-side data linked to it (account, saves, wallets, collections, chat messages, telemetry). As a fallback — for example if you have uninstalled the app — you can also email us at hello@optionalindustries.com, and we will fulfil deletion and export requests within 30 days.

7. Children

numbup Alpha is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

8. International Transfers

Game data is stored within the EU (Ireland). No transfers to third countries take place under the current Alpha scope. If crash reporting (Sentry) is activated with US hosting, this section will be updated with the applicable transfer mechanism (EU-US Data Privacy Framework / SCCs) before activation.

9. Cookies, Tracking, and Analytics

The App does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party advertising/tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The App does collect first-party gameplay telemetry linked to your anonymous account ID, as described in §2 and §3 — used solely to improve numbup, never for advertising, and never shared for marketing purposes.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify Alpha testers via the in-app World Chat or email if this policy changes materially. Continued use of the App after such notice constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
