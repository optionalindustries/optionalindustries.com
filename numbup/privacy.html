4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Technical processors who handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): Database, authentication, and realtime infrastructure. Project hosted in the EU (Ireland, AWS eu-west-1). Supabase Privacy Policy. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): Crash and performance reporting — active. Events are sent to Sentry's EU region (Germany); personal identifiers (display name, account ID, e-mail, tokens) are stripped on your device before the event leaves it.
  • Discord (Discord Inc.): When you report a chat message, that report is forwarded to a private moderation channel on Discord, so that a person sees it within 24 hours. Forwarded are the reported message text, the author's display name and the internal account IDs of author and reporter. Nothing else reaches Discord — ordinary chat messages, your save, your profile and your telemetry do not. Discord Inc. is a US company; see §8. Discord Privacy Policy
  • Apple: Distribution via TestFlight, subject to Apple's privacy policy. The Android build is distributed as a direct download (sideloaded APK) — no app store is involved and no store operator receives your data through it.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All game data is stored on EU-based servers (Supabase, Ireland).
  • Access is protected via Row Level Security — each account can only read its own data; chat messages are readable by all testers by design.
  • Server-side writes go through validated endpoints only; save data is integrity-protected (HMAC).
  • Session tokens are stored in the App's private storage area on your device. They are not encrypted at rest beyond what the operating system provides for app-private files; storing them in the platform keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) is planned before public release.
  • All network traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS).

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing or restrict it
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for Austria: dsb.gv.at)

Deletion and export are available in the app: open your profile, then Settings → Account, where you can Delete Account or Export My Data. Export gives you a machine-readable JSON copy of your data via the clipboard (a portable format, GDPR Art. 20). In-app deletion takes effect immediately and is irreversible — deleting your account removes all server-side data linked to it (account, saves, wallets, collections, chat messages, telemetry). Two rights currently have no button and run by email: if you want to object to gameplay telemetry (Art. 21, because we collect it on the basis of legitimate interest), or to be hidden from the Power Rankings, write to us and we will delete the telemetry we hold for your account and take you off the list. An in-app switch for both is planned.

As a fallback — for example if you have uninstalled the app — you can also email us at hello@optionalindustries.com, and we will fulfil deletion and export requests within 30 days.

7. Children

numbup is not made for children. The minimum age to use it is set out in our Terms of Service §3, and the age rating shown on the app's store page applies in addition.

Because accounts are created anonymously, we never ask for a date of birth — so we hold no age data and cannot verify anyone's age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has created an account, write to hello@optionalindustries.com and we will delete the account and everything linked to it, the same way we handle any other deletion request (see §6).

8. International Transfers

Game data is stored within the EU (Ireland). Crash and performance reports are stored in the EU (Germany). Sentry's operator is a US company, so access from outside the EU cannot be ruled out for support purposes; that transfer is covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses in Sentry's Data Processing Addendum.

Reported chat messages are transferred to Discord (see §4) and are therefore processed in the United States. This affects reports only — not ordinary chat, and nothing else in your account.

9. Cookies, Tracking, and Analytics

The App does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party advertising/tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The App does collect first-party gameplay telemetry linked to your anonymous account ID, as described in §2 and §3 — used solely to improve numbup, never for advertising, and never shared for marketing purposes.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy or our Terms change materially, the consent screen from your first app start appears again on the next start, showing the new version — that, not a chat message, is the notification. Continued use of the App after it constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
